Practical Safety Training

Dental Office Safety Procedures & Staff Responsibilities

Dental teams work in fast-paced environments where exposure risks can change throughout the day. Our training helps staff understand their day-to-day safety responsibilities, follow written procedures and respond appropriately to workplace incidents.

  • Follow written safety procedures
  • Respond appropriately to exposure incidents
  • Use PPE correctly during patient care
  • Handle chemicals and sharps safely
  • Understand SDS and hazard communication

Important Information

OSHA Medical Services and First Aid Requirements

Workplaces that rely on trained staff for emergency response should connect certification with broader safety planning. OSHA's first-aid rule covers trained personnel and readily available first-aid supplies when outside medical treatment is not in near proximity.

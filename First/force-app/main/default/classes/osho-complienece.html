Manufacturing Safety

Lockout/Tagout and Machine Safety Training

Manufacturing equipment can expose employees to moving parts and hazardous stored energy during servicing, setup, and maintenance. Training should help workers understand safe lockout/tagout procedures, machine guarding expectations, and the steps needed to reduce the risk of serious injuries.

Injury Prevention

Ergonomics Training and Injury Prevention

Repetitive motion, lifting, awkward postures, and forceful tasks can all contribute to workplace injuries in manufacturing settings. Therefore, ergonomics training helps employees recognize risk factors and apply safer work practices that support long-term injury prevention.

Chemical Safety

Hazard Communication Training & SDS Awareness

Employees should know how to identify chemical hazards, read labels, and access Safety Data Sheets relevant to their work area. This training supports safer chemical handling and reinforces hazard communication practices across the facility.

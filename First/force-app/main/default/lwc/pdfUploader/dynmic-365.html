    Working Together

    Procedure for a Smooth, Secure Engagement

    Here's exactly how access, communication and progress tracking work once your consultant starts.

    Your Dataverse, Azure and CRM environments stay yours. We never ask for data or credentials outside the project scope — access is scoped to the task, logged, and revoked at go-live. Bluethink follows ISO 27001 and GDPR-aligned practices for every client environment we touch.

    Cloud / SSO Access

    We connect through Azure AD guest access or your SSO provider and work inside your tenant under role-based permissions you set — nothing leaves your environment.

    VPN & RDP Access

    For on-prem systems or legacy CRM, we work through a secured VPN or remote desktop session you provision, and sign off the moment the session ends.

    We work in whatever tools your team already runs — Microsoft Teams, Slack, email or SharePoint — with a standing weekly sync so priorities never drift between calls.

    Response Time

    Questions raised during your business hours get a same-day reply; anything urgent is flagged within the hour.

    Time-Zone Overlap

    Your consultant works your shift hours or an agreed overlap window, confirmed with you before the engagement starts.

    You always know where the project stands — no waiting for a monthly check-in to find out what shipped.

    Progress Reporting

    Daily task logs plus a weekly status note covering what shipped, what's blocked, and what's next.

    Review Cadence

    A recurring review call — weekly for active builds, fortnightly for support — where you can reprioritize the backlog directly.
